# SECURITY_CHECKLIST.md — Around Privacy, Security & Governance Checklist

This checklist tracks database-enforced authorization, dual-layer privacy invariants, and operational safety controls across Around's eight capability domains.

| # | Security & Privacy Requirement | Database / RLS Enforcement (`supabase/`) | Client / Repository Enforcement (`lib/`) | Verification Suite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Friends-only Drops are inaccessible to strangers, anon users, and pending requesters (`SEC-01`)** | `posts_select_dual_layer` (`00002`) + `get_friends_feed` (`00003`/`00005`, `SECURITY INVOKER` bound to `auth.uid()`) | `PostsNotifier.getFriendsDrops` & `getDropsForPlace` (`lib/data/repositories/posts_repository.dart`) | `supabase/tests/rls_authorization_test.sql` (Tests 1, 2A, 8), `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart` |
| **2** | **Friend-request initiators cannot accept their own requests (`SEC-02`)** | `friendships_update_recipient_only` (`USING (auth.uid() = addressee_id)`) + `enforce_friendship_transition` trigger (`00005`) | `FriendshipStatusesNotifier` (`lib/data/repositories/profiles_repository.dart`) | `supabase/tests/rls_authorization_test.sql` (Test 2B), `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart` |
| **3** | **Only accepted mutual connections receive friends-only access across Posts, Collections, Stories & Plans** | `public.are_friends(user_a, user_b)` checks `status = 'accepted'` across `posts`, `collections`, `place_stories`, `friend_plans` | `FollowedFriendsNotifier` only includes `FriendshipRelation.accepted` | `supabase/tests/rls_authorization_test.sql` (Test 3) |
| **4** | **Bidirectional blocking immediately revokes content and interaction visibility** | `NOT public.is_blocked(auth.uid(), author_id)` in `posts`, `collections`, `place_stories`, `post_comments`, and `get_friends_feed` | `BlockedUsersNotifier` filters blocked users across all feeds and place pages | `supabase/tests/rls_authorization_test.sql` (Test 4), `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart` |
| **5** | **Chronological visit history is suppressed when `hide_visits_history = true` (`SEC-05`)** | `public.get_user_visible_drops(target_user_id)` RPC checks `hide_visits_history` (`00003`) | `canViewMemberVisitHistory` in `profiles_repository.dart` & `UserProfileScreen` | `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart`, `src/__tests__/privacy.test.ts` |
| **6** | **Users cannot modify another user's profile or write Drops/comments on behalf of another user** | `profiles_update_own`, `posts_insert_own`, `post_comments_insert_own` (`WITH CHECK (auth.uid() = author_id)`) | Repositories bind writes to `SupabaseService.currentUser!.id` | `supabase/tests/rls_authorization_test.sql` (Tests 5 & 6) |
| **7** | **Media uploads are validated for MIME type, 5 MB limit, and dual-layer Storage RLS (`SEC-06`)** | `post-media` bucket config (`file_size_limit = 5242880`, allowed MIME types) + `storage.objects` RLS (`00004`) | `AppLogger.validateImagePath` + `SupabaseService.uploadDropMedia` + `removeOrphanedMedia` | `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart` |
| **8** | **Database & client rate limits prevent Drop and Report flooding (`SEC-08`)** | `enforce_post_rate_limit` (max 10/5m) and `enforce_report_rate_limit` (max 15/5m) triggers (`00004`) | `PostsNotifier.publishDrop` sliding-window limiter | `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart` |
| **9** | **Community reports trigger automated quarantine at 3 reports and write to an audit log** | `handle_post_report_quarantine` (`00004`) + `log_post_quarantine_audit` (`00006` -> `moderation_audit_logs`) | `PostsNotifier.reportPost` (`posts_repository.dart`) | `test/unit/post_privacy_test.dart`, `src/__tests__/privacy.test.ts` |
| **10** | **Account deletion executes server-side without a client `service_role` key (`TASK-09`)** | `public.delete_own_account()` `SECURITY DEFINER` RPC bound to `auth.uid()` (`00005`) | `SupabaseService.deleteOwnAccount()` + `service_role` guard in `initialize()` | Verified in `00005` & `supabase_service.dart` |
| **11** | **Personalized discovery never uses private (`friends`-only) activity as global training/ranking input** | `public.get_recommended_places` (`00006`) is `SECURITY INVOKER` and only counts drops from the caller's own accepted friends | `PlacesRepository.getRecommendedPlaces` (`places_repository.dart`) | Verified in `00006_platform_domains_and_discovery.sql` |
